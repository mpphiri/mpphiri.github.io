## Summary

We study three-dimensional unforced Navier–Stokes flows using time-weighted inequalities between lower- and higher-order derivative norms. Interpreting derivative ratios as turbulence scales, we compare the radius of spatial analyticity with geometric sparseness scales. Under the paper's hypotheses, harmonic-measure estimates yield a contraction that excludes a class of strongly turbulent blow-up scenarios and allows analytic continuation beyond the candidate singular time.
